Emoji embeddings for “From the picture”

Runs the text half of MobileCLIP S0 over the description of every emoji “From the picture” can choose (the palette in src/masks/emojiData.js and the extra emojis in src/masks/pictureEmojis.js) and produces src/masks/emojiEmbeddings.json, so the masker only has to download the image half. Regenerate it whenever either list changes: open this page, wait, press Download and replace the file. Downloads about 170 MB the first time. The result is also in window.__emojiEmbeddings.
